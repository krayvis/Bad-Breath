Bad Breath: breath and dead air reduction for voiceover

This is a demo, tuned to one voice. The detection thresholds were fitted to a single narrator in one room. On other voices and rooms it will miss breaths and flag the wrong things, so treat every flag as a suggestion and review before exporting. Your audio stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
